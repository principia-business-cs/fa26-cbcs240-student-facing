# Rubric: Innovation Work

| Criterion | Strong Work | Developing Work | Needs More Work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | Uses specific evidence from people, field context, and technology | Uses some evidence, but leaves gaps | Relies mostly on opinion |
| Field Understanding | Shows clear knowledge of the field, constraints, and alternatives | Shows partial field knowledge | Field context is thin or missing |
| Insight | Frames a meaningful opportunity with nuance | Identifies a general problem | Problem is vague or unsupported |
| Experimentation | Uses prototypes or tests to learn | Tests something, but learning is limited | Prototype/test is missing or decorative |
| Agility | Adjusts based on evidence | Makes limited adjustments | Defends first idea regardless of evidence |
| Business Reasoning | Explains value, adoption, costs, revenue, and alternatives | Covers some business logic | Business model is unclear |
| Tradeoffs | Analyzes risks, opportunity cost, stress, money, and life implications | Mentions tradeoffs generally | Ignores or minimizes tradeoffs |
| Communication | Clear, organized, and persuasive | Understandable but uneven | Hard to follow |
