# Assignments

## Innovation Track Reflection

After the opening Letters From the Future activity, write a reflection on the innovation track you are most drawn to and the tradeoffs you may underestimate.

## Field Map

Create a map of the field connected to your innovation interest. Include people, customers, constraints, competitors, technologies, economics, and common failure modes.

## Discovery Portfolio

Document what you learn from interviews, observations, field research, and early opportunity exploration.

## Insights Report

Synthesize your discovery work into patterns, insights, a problem or opportunity frame, and a "How might we..." question.

## Track Tradeoff Memo

Analyze the opportunities, threats, risks, and opportunity costs connected to your chosen track.

## Prototype and Test Reports

Build low-fidelity prototypes or test artifacts. Each report should explain what assumption you tested, what happened, what you learned, and what changed.

## Business Model / Innovation Model

Explain how your innovation could create, deliver, and sustain value.

## Final Innovation Case

Present your opportunity, evidence, prototype learning, business reasoning, risks, tradeoffs, and recommended next step.
