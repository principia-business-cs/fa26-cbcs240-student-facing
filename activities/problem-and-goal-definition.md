# Activity: Problem and Goal Definition

## Purpose

You noticed things around campus that could become innovation opportunities. Today you will turn those observations into clearer problem and goal statements.

## Key Idea

Observation is not yet a problem.

A problem explains why the observation matters.

A goal describes what better would look like.

## Part 1: Share Your Observation

Use this format:

- I noticed...
- This might matter because...
- The people affected are...
- One possible improvement could be...

## Part 2: Ask Better Questions

When classmates share ideas, use curiosity before critique.

### Use “I wonder if...”

Examples:

- I wonder if this happens more at certain times of day.
- I wonder if first-year students experience this differently.
- I wonder if the real issue is information, motivation, access, or habit.
- I wonder if people already have a workaround.
- I wonder if technology would actually help.
- I wonder if a non-technology solution would work better.

### Use “Yes, and...”

Examples:

- Yes, and this could also affect people who...
- Yes, and we could test that by...
- Yes, and another version of the goal might be...
- Yes, and the first small prototype could be...
