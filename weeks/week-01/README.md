# Week 1 - Noticing, Introductions, Problem, and Goal

## Focus

This week we begin CBCS240 by getting to know each other, noticing possible innovation opportunities around campus, and learning how to turn observations into clearer problem and goal statements.

## Core Ideas

- Innovation starts with noticing.
- Observation is not yet a problem.
- A problem explains why the observation matters.
- A goal describes what better would look like.
- Good questions can make early ideas stronger.

## What You Will Do

1. Introduce yourself through a problem you notice.
2. Explore innovation tracks.
3. Write a short note to your future self.
4. Share campus observations.
5. Ask "I wonder if..." and "Yes, and..." questions.
6. Draft a problem statement and goal statement.
7. Identify one small test you could run next.
