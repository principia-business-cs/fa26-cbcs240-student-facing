# First Day Introductions: Innovation Lives

## Purpose

Today we are getting to know each other through the lens of this course:

> Innovation is not only about products or startups. It is about noticing problems, learning from people, experimenting, and building a future.

You do not need to have a business idea. You do not need to know your career path. Start with what you notice, what you care about, and what kind of future you are curious about.

## Part 1: The Problem I Notice

Take 3 minutes to think quietly and write short answers.

1. What is one small problem you notice in daily life, campus life, work, family, church, sports, your community, or online life?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Who experiences this problem?
4. What have people already tried to do about it?

## Introduction Format

When it is your turn, introduce yourself using this structure:

> My name is __________.  
> One problem I keep noticing is __________.  
> I think it matters because __________.  
> The people most affected are __________.

## Part 2: Choose an Innovation Track

Move to the part of the room that best matches the innovation path you are most curious about right now.

You can change your mind later.

## Track Options

**Investor / Evaluator**  
I am interested in spotting good ideas, markets, companies, products, or technologies before other people fully understand them.

**Local Business Builder**  
I am interested in building or improving a practical business in a real community, such as services, trades, food, retail, repair, or local operations.

**Long-Term Wealth Builder**  
I am interested in using discipline, career growth, saving, investing, and technology awareness to build stability over time.

**Platform Founder**  
I am interested in building something that can scale through software, networks, marketplaces, data, or AI.

**Product Creator**  
I am interested in creating a physical product, digital product, brand, tool, app, or service that people can use.

**Technology Innovator**  
I am interested in working directly with emerging technologies such as AI, automation, software, robotics, data, biotech, fintech, or other technical fields.

**Still Exploring**  
I am not sure yet, but I am curious.

## Part 3: Small Group Conversation

In your track group, discuss:

1. Why did you choose this track?
2. What excites you about it?
3. What worries you about it?
4. What kind of money, stress, freedom, family life, or community impact might come with this path?
5. What would you need to learn to pursue this path wisely?

Choose one person to summarize your group.

## Part 4: Dear Future Me

Write a short note to your future self.

Use this prompt:

> Ten years from now, I hope I am using technology and innovation to...

Then complete these sentences:

1. I hope my work feels...
2. I hope my life has room for...
3. I hope I have learned how to...
4. One tradeoff I want to be careful about is...
5. One thing I want to explore in this course is...
