# AI, Innovation, and Skills

## Purpose

This activity connects the Andrew Ng / Silicon Valley Girl interview to your own skills. As you watch, notice what people are building with AI, what skills they need, and what skills you may want to develop for your own future.

## 1. Watch For Skills

As you watch the interview, pay attention to what people are able to do with AI and what human skills still matter.

Discuss:

1. What did employees in the video build with AI?
2. What problem were they trying to solve?
3. How did AI help them move faster or do something they could not easily do before?
4. What human knowledge or judgment did they still need?
5. What risk, tradeoff, or opportunity did you notice?

## 2. Skill Spotting Game

In your group, make the longest useful list you can of skills you noticed in the video. You get one point for each real skill your group can explain with evidence from the interview.

A skill only counts if your group can answer:

```text
Where did we notice this skill in the video?
Why did that skill matter?
```

Possible skills:

- Asking good questions
- Understanding a field or industry
- Building prototypes
- Using AI tools
- Communicating ideas clearly
- Testing whether something works
- Spotting risks and tradeoffs
- Learning quickly
- Working with people
- Making decisions with incomplete information

## 5. How Do People Actually Acquire Skills?

Pick one skill you want to build. Then answer:

1. What would practice look like for this skill?
2. Who could teach, coach, or model this skill for me?
3. What small project could force me to use this skill?
4. How could AI help me practice or improve this skill?
5. How will I know I am getting better?
