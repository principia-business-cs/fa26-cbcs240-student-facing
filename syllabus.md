# CBCS240 Technology and Innovation - Syllabus

## Course Description

CBCS240 Technology and Innovation is an experiential course about how people create, evaluate, and adapt new value using technology.

The course uses a track-based approach. You will compare different innovation paths: investing in new products, building practical community businesses, saving and investing over time, creating platform companies, developing products, and working directly with emerging technologies.

Across these tracks, you will practice design thinking, field discovery, experimentation, business reasoning, and reflection on the tradeoffs different futures require.

## Core Question

How do people create, evaluate, fund, adopt, and scale new value through technology, and what tradeoffs do those paths create in a real life?

## Course Loop

We will repeatedly practice this loop:

1. Goal
2. Data
3. Experiment
4. Sensemaking
5. Adjustment

## Major Work

- Innovation Track Reflection
- Field Map
- Discovery Portfolio
- Insights Report
- Track Tradeoff Memo
- Prototype and Test Reports
- Business Model / Innovation Model
- Final Innovation Case
- Final Presentation

## Assessment Categories

Your work will be evaluated on:

- Evidence from people, markets, technology, and field context
- Quality of insight and problem framing
- Agility and responsiveness to new evidence
- Prototype design and learning
- Business reasoning
- Awareness of risk, threats, opportunity cost, and tradeoffs
- Communication clarity
- Individual contribution and team collaboration
