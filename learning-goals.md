# Learning Goals

By the end of CBCS240, you should be able to:

- Recognize opportunities created by unmet needs, field changes, technology shifts, and community problems.
- Learn from people through observation, interviews, and feedback.
- Understand the field you are innovating in, including customers, constraints, competitors, economics, and common failure modes.
- Frame and reframe problems based on evidence.
- Generate multiple possible solutions before committing to one.
- Build and test low-fidelity prototypes.
- Interpret feedback without becoming defensive or over-attached to a first idea.
- Evaluate opportunities, threats, risks, and opportunity costs.
- Explain the business logic of an innovation.
- Compare innovation tracks and describe their tradeoffs for money, stress, family, community, meaning, and time.
- Present an innovation case clearly through writing, visuals, and speech.
