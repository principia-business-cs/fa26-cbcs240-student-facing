# CBCS240 Technology and Innovation

Welcome to CBCS240 Technology and Innovation.

This course is about how people create, evaluate, test, and adapt new value using technology. We will study innovation as something practical and human: a way of learning, making decisions, experimenting, and building a future.

## Course Idea

Innovation is not a perfect plan that moves in a straight line. In this course, you will practice an agile learning loop:

1. Set a goal.
2. Gather data.
3. Experiment.
4. Make sense of what happened.
5. Adjust.

## Innovation Tracks

You will explore innovation through several possible paths:

- Investor / evaluator
- Local business builder
- Long-term wealth builder
- Platform founder
- Product creator
- Technology innovator

These tracks are not boxes you must stay inside forever. They are lenses for thinking about different kinds of opportunity, risk, money, stress, family life, community impact, and future work.
